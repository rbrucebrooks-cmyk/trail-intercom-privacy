Trail Intercom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Trail Intercom ("the app") is an activity tracker (walks, hikes, runs, rides, paddles) with a built-in voice intercom and rider map for small groups who are often out of cellular range. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Trail Intercom has no user accounts, no servers, and no analytics. It does not collect your personal information, and it does not send any of your data to the developer or to any third party. Your recorded activities stay on your phone (and in your own Google account backup, if you have Android backup turned on). Everything the app transmits during a group session goes directly between the phones in your group over a local Wi-Fi connection that the phones create between themselves. Sharing with Health Connect is optional and under your control.

Information the app uses, and why

  • Microphone (voice): While you are in an intercom session, your microphone audio is streamed in real time to the other riders in your group so you can talk to each other. Audio is not recorded, not saved, and not sent to the developer or any server. When the session ends, the audio stream stops.
  • Location: While an activity is being recorded, your device's GPS position is used to build the track of your outing (distance, pace, climb, map, mile splits). In a group session, if you leave location sharing turned on (you can turn it off in settings), your position is also shared with the other riders in your group so everyone appears on the in-app map, and theirs with you. Positions are never sent to the developer or any server. See "Activity history" below for what is kept.
  • Watch heart rate: If you install the companion Wear OS app, your watch's heart-rate sensor reading is sent to your phone during an activity, shown on screen, and saved with the outing. In a group session you can choose whether to share it with the other riders; it is never sent to the developer or any server.
  • Bluetooth: Used to route audio to and from a Bluetooth headset or earbuds you choose to connect, and, if you pair one, to send ride stats (speed, distance, time, heart rate, and other riders' names with their distance and direction from you) to your own bike computer display. That data goes directly from your phone to the display and is not stored on it.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices: Used only to create and join the direct phone-to-phone Wi-Fi connection that carries the intercom and map data.
  • Diagnostic logs: The app writes technical diagnostic logs (connection events, signal-quality statistics, timing, and rider display names) to storage on your device to help troubleshoot connection problems. These logs may be shared over the local Wi-Fi connection with the "host" phone in your group so the group's technical issues can be reviewed together. The logs do not contain your conversation audio and are never sent to the developer or any server. They remain on the devices in your group.

Activity history

Each outing you record (your GPS track with time, speed, altitude and heart rate, plus the tracks and heart rates of other riders in a group session as they were shared with you) is saved as a file on your phone so you can review it later. This history is stored only on your device. It is included in Android's backup to your own Google account if you have device backup turned on, and you can export it as a file or as GPX from within the app to any destination you choose. You can delete any outing, or all of them, at any time in the app.

Health Connect (optional)

If you turn on Sync outings to Health Connect in the app's settings, Trail Intercom writes each of your finished outings into Android's Health Connect so that other health and fitness apps on your phone (for example Fitbit or Google Fit) can show it. What is written is an exercise session with its type, your GPS route, distance, elevation gained, an estimate of calories, and the heart rate recorded during the outing. Only your own data is written. Positions and heart rates that other riders shared with you in a group session are never written to Health Connect.

Trail Intercom reads from Health Connect for one purpose only: when one of your outings has no heart rate (for example because the watch app was not running), it reads heart-rate samples that other apps recorded during that outing's time window and attaches them to the outing. It does not read any other data type, and it does not read heart rate outside your own outings.

Health Connect data is stored on your phone under Android's own controls. The app does not send Health Connect data to the developer or to any server, and does not use it for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than showing and recording your own activities. You can turn sync off in the app at any time and revoke the app's access in Health Connect; data already written remains in Health Connect until you delete it there or delete the outing in the app, which removes its Health Connect copy.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with any third party. There are no advertising or analytics services in the app.

Data retention

Live audio is not retained. Recorded outings are kept on your device until you delete them in the app. Diagnostic logs are kept on the device and are automatically rotated, with only the most recent sessions retained. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored on the phone; data you chose to write to Health Connect is governed by Health Connect.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13.
