Trail Intercom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Trail Intercom ("the app") is a voice intercom and rider-location app for small groups of cyclists and other outdoor users who are often out of cellular range. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Trail Intercom has no user accounts, no servers, and no analytics. It does not collect your personal information, and it does not send any of your data to the developer or to any third party. Everything the app transmits goes directly between the phones in your riding group over a local Wi-Fi connection that the phones create between themselves.

Information the app uses, and why

  • Microphone (voice): While you are in an intercom session, your microphone audio is streamed in real time to the other riders in your group so you can talk to each other. Audio is not recorded, not saved, and not sent to the developer or any server. When the session ends, the audio stream stops.
  • Location: If you leave location sharing turned on (you can turn it off before joining), your device's GPS position is shared with the other riders in your group so everyone appears on the in-app map. Positions are used only during the active session, are not saved after the session ends, and are never sent to the developer or any server.
  • Bluetooth: Used only to route audio to and from a Bluetooth headset or earbuds you choose to connect.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices: Used only to create and join the direct phone-to-phone Wi-Fi connection that carries the intercom and map data.
  • Diagnostic logs: The app writes technical diagnostic logs (connection events, signal-quality statistics, timing, and rider display names) to storage on your device to help troubleshoot connection problems. These logs may be shared over the local Wi-Fi connection with the "host" phone in your group so the group's technical issues can be reviewed together. The logs do not contain your conversation audio and are never sent to the developer or any server. They remain on the devices in your group.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with any third party. There are no advertising or analytics services in the app.

Data retention

Live audio and location are not retained after a session ends. Diagnostic logs are kept on the device and are automatically rotated, with only the most recent sessions retained. You can clear them at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
